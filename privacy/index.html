This policy describes the Nick Channel desktop application used by its owner to connect that owner’s TikTok account and upload videos chosen on that owner’s computer. Nick Channel does not operate a separate account system and does not offer the app to the public as a hosted service.

Information used

When the owner authorizes Nick Channel through TikTok Login Kit, the app requests the basic profile permission. From that permission it receives the TikTok open ID, avatar, and display name of the authorized account. It uses those fields to confirm which account is connected.

When the owner uploads a video, Nick Channel uses the video file the owner selected and the caption or title the owner entered for that upload. A direct post uses the direct-post permission and is sent to the authorized user’s own TikTok profile. A draft upload uses the draft-upload permission and is sent to that account’s TikTok inbox.

The app also handles the TikTok authorization code, access token, and refresh token for that connection, and it stores the app’s client secret on the owner’s computer. These values are used to sign in and to send the upload the owner started.

How information is handled

Profile fields, tokens, and the client secret stay on the owner’s computer except when the app contacts TikTok to complete sign-in or an upload the owner started. The selected video is sent to TikTok for that upload. Nick Channel does not sell this information, does not use it for advertising, and does not send it to any other company for that company’s own purposes.

Nick Channel does not request the account biography, video list, messages, follows, or comments. It does not change the TikTok account’s privacy setting.

How long it is kept

Tokens and the client secret remain on the computer until the owner deletes them or revokes Nick Channel in TikTok. A video file remains wherever the owner stored it. After an upload, TikTok handles the posted or draft video under TikTok’s own privacy policy.

Choice and revocation

The owner can refuse TikTok’s authorization prompt, skip an upload, or revoke Nick Channel’s access from the TikTok account settings. Revoking access stops the app from making new requests with that authorization.
